## Business Understanding

This course was designed to give learners a structured, end-to-end understanding of how AI/ML is applied in computational biology and bioinformatics — starting from fundamental concepts and data principles, moving through practical data science and modeling workflows, and finishing with deep learning and emerging no-code/agentic AI approaches specific to the life sciences.

## Approach

The course is organized as a progressive, six-module curriculum:

1. **Module 1 — AI/ML Foundations**: Build core understanding of AI/ML concepts and real-world applications.
2. **Module 2 — Data Science Life Cycle**: Learn the FAIR data principles and responsible, data-centric AI/ML practices.
3. **Module 3 — Data Preparation**: Practice data collection, feature engineering, scaling, and feature selection.
4. **Module 4 — Modeling**: Build, evaluate, tune, interpret, and deploy ML models.
5. **Module 5 — Deep Learning**: Introduce deep learning theory and its applications in computational biology and bioinformatics.
6. **Module 6 — No-Code and Agentic AI**: Explore no-code and agentic AI tools tailored to computational biology and bioinformatics workflows.
